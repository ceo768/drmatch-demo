About the Doctor

Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo is a board-certified dermatologist at Gangnam Thema Root Hair Transplant in Seoul, known for female hairline design, non-incisional (FUE) hair transplant and large sessions of over 7,000 grafts. With more than 25 years of experience, Dr. Lee works with a six-team collaborative system that shortens the time harvested follicles spend outside the body. STMSTYLE has collaborated with Dr. Lee for over 15 years.

Case Expertise

Female Hairline Design

More than narrowing the forehead, the position and shape of the hairline are designed from the proportion of the whole face, taking hair direction and density into account so the transition looks natural.

Non-incisional Hair Transplant (FUE)

Follicles are harvested one by one from the back of the head and implanted. No linear scar remains, so short hair is possible, and the work is done quickly in teams with follicle survival time in mind.

Large-graft Transplant (7,000+ grafts)

For cases needing a large number of grafts in one session, six teams work simultaneously to shorten the time from harvesting to implantation and minimise how long follicles remain outside the body.

Philosophy of Care

Dr. Lee believes a hair transplant result depends not on one surgeon's technique alone but on the speed and teamwork of the whole process of harvesting, dissecting and implanting follicles. Six teams work closely together with follicle survival time in mind, so that a large number of grafts can be implanted reliably in a short time. A female hairline is designed naturally around the proportion of the whole face, hair direction and density; incisional or non-incisional methods are chosen to suit the patient, and long-term aftercare is part of the commitment.

— Lee Yoon-Joo

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo experienced in female hairline transplants?

Yes. Female hairline design is one of Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo's main procedures. Rather than simply narrowing the forehead, Dr. Lee designs the hairline from the proportion of the whole face, considering hair direction and density for a natural look. The final plan is set after an in-person consultation.

Can Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo perform large hair transplant sessions?

Yes. Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo performs sessions of over 7,000 grafts using a six-team system that works simultaneously to shorten the time from harvesting to implantation. The right number of grafts depends on each person's hair loss and donor area.

Which hair transplant methods does Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo use?

Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo performs both incisional (FUT) and non-incisional (FUE) hair transplants, choosing the method according to the patient's condition. FUE leaves no linear scar, so short hair is possible. Dr. Lee also performs transplants to various other areas of the body.

How long should I stay in Korea for a hair transplant with Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo?

The standard stay for a hair transplant with Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo is 8 days and 7 nights, adjusted for large-graft sessions and recovery. The clinic guides patients step by step on shampooing, disinfection and graft care so aftercare continues after returning home.

Can I consult Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo through STMSTYLE in Thailand?

Yes. STMSTYLE, which has worked with Dr. Lee Yoon-Joo for over 15 years, coordinates initial consultations from Bangkok via LINE or WhatsApp and confirms schedules and conditions with the clinic. The final decision on method and graft count is made after an in-person consultation with the doctor.
