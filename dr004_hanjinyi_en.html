About the Doctor

Dr. Han Jin Yi is a board-certified plastic surgeon and director at Gangnam Hera Plastic Surgery in Seoul, known for Ulthera and Thermage lifting, lip filler and anatomy-based filler design for specialised areas. With 22 years of experience and multiple published studies on facial and soft-tissue anatomy, Dr. Han combines filler, threads, lasers and skin boosters only as far as needed to achieve a natural, balanced result.

Case Expertise

Ulthera and Thermage

Ultrasound and radiofrequency energy are delivered to deep skin layers to improve firmness. The skeleton, fat layers and retaining ligaments are assessed first to set depth and area, and devices are combined according to skin condition.

Lip Filler

Adjusts lip fullness and outline, considering philtrum length and proportion to the nose and chin together when deciding placement and volume.

Anatomy-based Special-Area Filler and Lifting Design

Treats areas with complex structure or where vessel courses require care, such as the under-eye, neck and brow ridge, judging layer and depth from anatomical research, and designs facial lifting around each patient's structure.

Philosophy of Care

Anatomy comes before the procedure. The skeleton, fat layers, retaining ligaments and soft tissue are analysed together with facial proportion to design a balance that is natural rather than excessive.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Han Jin Yi good at lip filler?

Yes. Lip filler is one of Dr. Han Jin Yi's main treatments. Placement and volume are decided by considering philtrum length and proportion to the nose and chin, not the lips alone.

What kind of patients is Dr. Han Jin Yi best suited for?

Dr. Han Jin Yi is well suited to patients who want a more defined, balanced face through lifting, filler or laser treatments rather than surgery. The skeleton, fat layers and ligaments are analysed before treatments are combined. Suitability is confirmed at an in-person consultation.

How long should I stay in Korea for treatment with Dr. Han Jin Yi?

The recommended stay for treatment with Dr. Han Jin Yi is 1–2 days. If several areas are combined, the clinic reviews overlapping treatments before giving one plan, so please share your travel dates in advance.

What fillers and botulinum toxin does Dr. Han Jin Yi use?

Dr. Han Jin Yi uses both Korean and imported fillers, and Korean and German botulinum toxin, including a formulation with complexing proteins removed to reduce the chance of antibody formation with repeated treatment. MINT and MINT Easy threads are used for lifting.

Does Dr. Han Jin Yi treat patients personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Yes, Dr. Han Jin Yi performs treatments personally as a director of the clinic. Patients can begin a consultation through STMSTYLE in Bangkok via LINE or WhatsApp, and STMSTYLE confirms schedules and conditions with the clinic. The final plan and cost are set after an in-person consultation.
