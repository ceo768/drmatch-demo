About the Doctor

Dr. Barn Jaesang is a board-certified plastic surgeon at Banobagi Plastic Surgery in Seoul, known for stem-cell and regenerative treatment combined with fat-based surgery such as liposuction and gynaecomastia correction. A Seoul National University MD and PhD with 30 years in practice, he has performed more than 10,000 breast operations according to Banobagi's published data. The hospital runs its own stem-cell research centre so that cell processing is managed in-house.

Case Expertise

Stem-cell and Regenerative Treatment

Treatment applying cells obtained from fat and other sources after processing. On the view that how cells are obtained and processed determines the result, as much of the process as possible is managed through the hospital's own research centre and equipment.

Liposuction

Reduces fat in the areas required according to body shape, drawing on surgical experience in handling fat tissue, with harvested fat also used for regenerative purposes where appropriate.

Gynaecomastia

Corrects a male chest that has developed in a feminised form. The proportion of glandular tissue to fat is assessed first, before the extent of excision and suction is decided.

Breast Surgery

Breast surgery is one of his most extensive areas of experience, with more than 10,000 breast operations including approximately 2,000+ Motiva implant cases.

Philosophy of Care

His view is that stem-cell treatment is not a matter of offering a procedure by name, but of the infrastructure required to obtain, process and apply the cells. He invests actively in the relevant equipment and in-house systems, and values connecting surgical experience in handling fat tissue with regenerative medicine to raise consistency and expertise in treatment.

— Barn Jaesang

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Barn Jaesang experienced in stem-cell treatment?

Yes. Stem-cell and regenerative treatment is Dr. Barn Jaesang's main focus, and he serves as Standing Director of the Korean Society for Stem Cell and Tissue Regeneration. Banobagi runs its own stem-cell research centre, so cell harvesting and processing are managed in-house as far as possible. Suitability is assessed at an in-person consultation.

How experienced is Dr. Barn Jaesang in breast surgery?

According to Banobagi's published data, Dr. Barn Jaesang has performed more than 10,000 breast operations, including approximately 2,000+ Motiva implant cases, over 30 years in practice. The implant type and surgical method are decided after an in-person assessment.

What kind of patients is Dr. Barn Jaesang best suited for?

Dr. Barn Jaesang suits patients interested in stem-cell treatment who value equipment, cell-processing systems and physician expertise, as well as those seeking liposuction, gynaecomastia correction or breast surgery. He is able to connect surgical experience with fat tissue to regenerative medicine.

How long should I stay in Korea for surgery with Dr. Barn Jaesang?

The standard stay for treatment with Dr. Barn Jaesang is eight days and seven nights, adjusted according to the surgery. The exact length is confirmed once the treatment plan is set at an in-person consultation.

Can I consult Dr. Barn Jaesang through STMSTYLE in Thailand?

Yes. STMSTYLE has a long-term collaboration with Dr. Barn Jaesang and Banobagi, and coordinates initial consultations, scheduling and conditions with the hospital from Bangkok via LINE or WhatsApp. Surgery is performed by the doctor himself, and final decisions are made at an in-person consultation.
