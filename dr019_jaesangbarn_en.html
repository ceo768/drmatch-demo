Philosophy of Care
His view is that stem-cell treatment is not a matter of offering a procedure by name, but of the infrastructure required to obtain, process and apply the cells. He invests actively in the relevant equipment and in-house systems, and values connecting surgical experience in handling fat tissue with regenerative medicine to raise consistency and expertise in treatment.

Signature Treatments
Stem-cell and Regenerative Treatment
In-house systems
Treatment applying cells obtained from fat and other sources after processing. On the view that how cells are obtained and processed determines the result, as much of the process as possible is managed directly through the in-house research centre and equipment.
Liposuction
Area-by-area planning
Reduces fat in the areas required according to body shape. Carried out on the basis of surgical experience in handling fat tissue, with the harvested fat also used for regenerative purposes where appropriate.
Gynaecomastia
Male chest
Corrects a male chest that has developed in a feminised form. The proportion of glandular tissue to fat is assessed first, before the extent of excision and suction is decided.
The area, extent and number of procedures vary with each individual. The final plan and cost are determined after an in-person consultation. All procedures carry individual variation and possible side effects.
