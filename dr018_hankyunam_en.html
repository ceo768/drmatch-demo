Philosophy of Care
His view is that lifting is less about pulling a great deal than about improving descent and wrinkles naturally, in a way appropriate to the patient's age. In middle-aged and older patients in particular, anaesthesia and general condition are weighed as heavily as the result, and surgery is not pushed forward where safety standards are not sufficiently met. Suited to patients who want natural rejuvenation and safety together.

Signature Treatments
Facelift
SMAS layer, extending to the neck
Rather than pulling skin alone, the deep facial layer (SMAS) is lifted together to improve descent. The scope can extend to the neck where required, and the amount of lift is set by what is natural for the patient's age.
Mini Lift
Limited scope
Where descent is not extensive or a shorter recovery is preferred, lifting is carried out with reduced incision and dissection. The extent of improvement possible is explained precisely before the decision is made.
Minimal-incision Lifting
Minimising scar burden
Lifting carried out with incisions kept to a minimum in inconspicuous positions such as around the ear. The aim is to improve descent of the midface and lower face while reducing the burden of scarring.
The area, extent and number of procedures vary with each individual. The final plan and cost are determined after an in-person consultation. All procedures carry individual variation and possible side effects.
