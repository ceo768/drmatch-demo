Signature Treatments
He practises across the full range at Gangnam Hera — lifting and firming, botulinum toxin and filler, skin boosters and regeneration. What follows are the areas of particular focus.
Under-eye Filler
An area requiring vascular caution
Improves hollowing and shadow under the eye. Because the skin is thin and the course of vessels requires care, judgement of layer and depth is critical, and placement is decided on the basis of academic experience in blepharoplasty and facial anatomy.
Thread Lifting
Midface and lower face
Absorbable threads are used to lift descended tissue. The position of the retaining ligaments and the direction in which ageing has progressed are read first to set the direction and number of threads, with filler planned alongside where needed.
Midface Rejuvenation
Combined filler and thread
Addresses volume loss and descent across the midface, from below the cheekbone to the nasolabial fold. Rather than relying on one method, filler and thread are combined by reading the skeleton and fat compartments.
For botulinum toxin, a German formulation with complexing proteins removed is used alongside, taking into account the possibility of antibody formation with repeated treatment.

The area, extent and number of procedures vary with each individual. The final plan and cost are determined after an in-person consultation. All procedures carry individual variation and possible side effects.
