About the Doctor

Dr. Kim Jong Seon is a board-certified plastic surgeon and director at Gangnam Hera Plastic Surgery in Seoul, known for under-eye filler, thread lifting and midface rejuvenation that combines filler with threads. He serves as a board director of the Korean Society of Aesthetic Surgery (KSAS) and has presented and taught on blepharoplasty, facial anatomy, filler and thread lifting since 2012.

Case Expertise

Under-Eye Filler

Improves under-eye hollowing and shadow. Because the skin is thin and vessels require care, layer and depth are judged precisely, with placement guided by academic experience in blepharoplasty and facial anatomy.

Thread Lifting

Absorbable threads lift descended tissue in the midface and lower face. Retaining ligaments and the direction of ageing are read first to set thread direction and number, with filler planned alongside where needed.

Special-Area Filler and Midface Rejuvenation

Addresses volume loss and descent from below the cheekbone to the nasolabial fold, as well as marionette lines, neck lines and lips, combining filler and threads based on the skeleton and fat compartments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Kim Jong Seon good at under-eye filler?

Yes. Under-eye filler is one of Dr. Kim Jong Seon's main treatments. He sets injection layer and depth carefully because under-eye skin is thin and vessels require caution, drawing on academic experience in blepharoplasty and facial anatomy.

What kind of patients is Dr. Kim Jong Seon best suited for?

Dr. Kim Jong Seon is well suited to patients with under-eye hollows, sagging cheeks or midface volume loss who want a natural, balanced result through minimally invasive treatment. He often combines filler and threads according to each patient's facial structure. Suitability is confirmed at an in-person consultation.

How long should I stay in Korea for treatment with Dr. Kim Jong Seon?

The recommended stay for treatment with Dr. Kim Jong Seon is 1–2 days. If several areas are combined, the clinic reviews overlapping treatments before giving one plan, so please share your previous filler and thread history and travel dates in advance.

What threads and fillers does Dr. Kim Jong Seon use?

Dr. Kim Jong Seon uses MINT and MINT Easy threads for lifting, Korean and imported fillers, and Korean and German botulinum toxin, including a formulation with complexing proteins removed to reduce the chance of antibody formation with repeated treatment.

Does Dr. Kim Jong Seon treat patients personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Yes, Dr. Kim Jong Seon performs treatments personally as a director of the clinic. Patients can begin a consultation through STMSTYLE in Bangkok via LINE or WhatsApp, and STMSTYLE confirms schedules and conditions with the clinic. The final plan and cost are set after an in-person consultation.
