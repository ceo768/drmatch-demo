About the Doctor

Dr. Yang Hae-Won is a board-certified plastic surgeon at INIQUE Plastic Surgery in Gangnam, Seoul, known for complex revision eyelid surgery, lower eyelid fat repositioning and nanofat grafting for thin skin. Trained in plastic surgery at Samsung Medical Center, he has more than 17 years of experience and over 15,000 cases. His work focuses on natural results and meticulous bleeding control to reduce swelling and recovery demands.

Case Expertise

Complex Revision Eyelid Surgery

Corrects crease problems, adhesion and asymmetry left by previous surgery, including high, thick eyelid creases. Even after multiple operations with thinned tissue or severe adhesion, how the tissue has healed is assessed first before the extent of revision is decided.

Lower Eyelid Fat Repositioning

Instead of removing protruding under-eye fat, it is moved into the hollow so that shadow and irregularity are addressed together. A transconjunctival or open approach is chosen by skin condition, with nanofat grafting for thin skin.

Incisional Double Eyelid and Ptosis Correction

The crease is formed while the eye-opening muscle is adjusted, balancing definition with a natural look. Bleeding is carefully controlled to reduce swelling and recovery demands.

Philosophy of Care

Advanced technique must still deliver a natural result, so no small detail is overlooked, even in complex revision eye and lower eyelid surgery. Because heavier bleeding during eye surgery can increase swelling and recovery demands, he invests continuously in haemostatic and laser equipment and controls bleeding meticulously. For the under-eye area and thin, sensitive skin, nanofat and cell-supplemented grafting are used to smooth irregularities and blend naturally. Even minor concerns are re-examined, and retouching is taken on where needed.

— Yang Hae-Won

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Yang Hae-Won good at revision eyelid surgery?

Yes. Dr. Yang Hae-Won specialises in complex revision eyelid surgery, including over-corrected ptosis, asymmetry and high, thick eyelid creases. He assesses adhesion and tissue condition from previous surgery before deciding the extent of revision. Suitability is confirmed at an in-person consultation.

Who might consider consulting Dr. Yang Hae-Won?

Dr. Yang Hae-Won is well suited to people who want a natural, soft eye result, those needing revision after previous eye surgery, and those with under-eye bags or hollows. His approach is preservation-focused, with meticulous bleeding control.

How long should I stay in Korea for eye surgery with Dr. Yang Hae-Won?

The recommended stay for surgery with Dr. Yang Hae-Won is about 7 days. The actual length may vary with the procedure and individual condition, and is confirmed after consultation.

What equipment and materials does Dr. Yang Hae-Won use?

Dr. Yang Hae-Won uses the Lumenis UltraPulse laser and dedicated laser equipment for bleeding control to reduce swelling and recovery demands. He also uses Endotine, Motiva breast implants, and a nanofat and cell-supplemented fat grafting system for the under-eye area and thin skin.

Does Dr. Yang Hae-Won operate personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Yes, Dr. Yang Hae-Won performs surgery personally as the clinic's director. You can begin a consultation through STMSTYLE in Bangkok via LINE or WhatsApp, and STMSTYLE confirms schedules and conditions with the clinic. The final plan and cost are set after an in-person consultation.
