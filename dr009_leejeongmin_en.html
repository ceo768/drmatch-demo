About the Doctor

Dr. Lee Jeong-Min is a board-certified plastic surgeon at URBAN Plastic Surgery in Gangnam, Seoul, known for incisional double eyelid surgery, ptosis correction, revision eye surgery and facelift. He has 16 years in practice since 2010 and 60,000–70,000 clinical cases, and completed a clinical fellowship at Seoul National University Hospital. He assesses the eyes together with the brow, forehead and midface to create a defined yet natural look that keeps the patient's own image.

Case Expertise

Incisional Double Eyelid and Ptosis Correction

The eyelid crease is formed while the strength of the muscle that opens the eye is adjusted. The height of the line is set by judging the position of the brow and forehead, not the eye alone.

Revision Eye Surgery

Corrects adhesion, crease problems and asymmetry caused by previous eye surgery. The tissue condition and degree of adhesion from the earlier operation are checked before the extent of revision is decided.

Facelift

Lifts facial descent, planned alongside eye surgery where descent of the brow, forehead or midface cannot be addressed by eye surgery alone, with fat grafting where required.

Philosophy of Care

He pursues natural change that keeps the patient's own image while harmonising with the whole face. The structure and present condition of the eye are assessed precisely and the direction of surgery is decided from the proportion and balance of the face; in revision, the tissue condition and degree of adhesion from previous surgery are examined closely. Rather than looking at the eye alone, the descent and volume of the brow, forehead and midface are judged together, and lifting or fat grafting is planned alongside where needed. What matters is the result that suits the patient best, not excessive change.

— Lee Jeong-Min

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Lee Jeong-Min good at revision eye surgery?

Yes. Dr. Lee Jeong-Min performs revision eye surgery and high-complexity cases, correcting adhesion, crease problems and asymmetry from previous surgery. He checks tissue condition and the degree of adhesion before deciding the extent of revision, which is confirmed after an in-person consultation.

What kind of patients is Dr. Lee Jeong-Min best suited for?

Dr. Lee Jeong-Min suits patients who want defined yet natural eyes that keep their own image, including those with a heavy or tired-looking eye, those needing correction of previous eye surgery, and those with descent of the brow, forehead or midface as well.

Can Dr. Lee Jeong-Min combine a facelift with eye surgery?

Yes. Dr. Lee Jeong-Min plans a facelift alongside eye surgery when descent of the brow, forehead or midface cannot be corrected by eye surgery alone, with fat grafting where needed. The combination is decided according to each patient's condition.

How long should I stay in Korea for surgery with Dr. Lee Jeong-Min?

The recommended stay for surgery with Dr. Lee Jeong-Min is 7–14 days, according to the type and number of procedures. The appropriate stay is advised individually after consultation.

Does Dr. Lee Jeong-Min perform the surgery personally, and how can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Surgery is performed by Dr. Lee Jeong-Min himself. STMSTYLE works directly with him and URBAN Plastic Surgery, coordinating from the first consultation in Bangkok or via WhatsApp through to scheduling at the clinic. The final plan and cost are confirmed after an in-person consultation.
