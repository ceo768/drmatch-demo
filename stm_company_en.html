How this content is made
This is not written as advertising copy. STM compiles content from material supplied by doctors and medical institutions, publicly disclosed records, related cases and the doctors' own explanations.

Complex Korean medical information is conveyed in English and Thai so that international patients can follow it. What has not been confirmed is not stated as fact.

We start from the patient's concern
Even for the same operation, each patient's condition and the change they want are different. So is each doctor's main field, way of judging, and the cases their experience has accumulated in.

STM starts not from medical terminology or promotions, but from what the patient actually feels. We connect that concern to the relevant doctors, treatment information and cases.
From information to the journey itself
STM's role does not end at passing on information. Consultation with Korean doctors, scheduling, hospital visits, interpreting, support during the stay, and follow-up after returning home are arranged around the patient's schedule and needs.

The scope of support varies with the patient and the treatment plan.
About STM
STMstyle was established in Bangkok in 2009 and has connected Korean medicine with international patients since. A multilingual team of Thai, Korean and Australian colleagues turns information about Korean doctors and their cases into content international patients can follow.
