About the Doctor

Dr. Whang Jong-Dae is a board-certified obstetrician and gynaecologist at Miyedam Obstetrics and Gynecology Clinic in Seoul, known for vaginal tightening surgery that accounts for post-childbirth change, labiaplasty and revision of female genital surgery. Trained at Seoul National University Hospital and Samsung Medical Center, he has more than 25 years in practice (since 1997) and over 10,000 cases, and considers function, form and recovery together.

Case Expertise

Vaginal Tightening Surgery

Tightens the vaginal canal and perineum together where they have loosened through childbirth or ageing. The muscle layer, mucosa and perineal form are assessed before the extent of surgery is set, and laser or implant-based methods are chosen according to tissue condition.

Labiaplasty (Labia Minora and Majora)

Corrects elongation and asymmetry of the labia minora and volume change in the labia majora, taking everyday discomfort such as friction or hygiene into account as well as aesthetic form.

Revision Female Genital Surgery

Corrects asymmetry, excessive excision, scarring or discomfort remaining after previous surgery. Tissue condition is checked before the extent of revision is decided, and a staged approach is explained in advance where one operation cannot resolve the issue.

Philosophy of Care

Based on accurate diagnosis, he recommends only the treatment that is needed, and places importance on thorough antisepsis and on reducing the burden of pain. Through obstetric training at Seoul National University Hospital he understands the structural changes and discomfort that follow pregnancy and childbirth, and decides the extent of surgery for each patient by considering function, form and recovery together. He oversees consultation, surgery and aftercare personally, and puts each patient's result and trust before promotion.

— Whang Jong-Dae

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Whang Jong-Dae experienced in vaginal tightening after childbirth?

Yes. Dr. Whang Jong-Dae is known for vaginal tightening surgery that accounts for post-childbirth change. Drawing on obstetric training at Seoul National University Hospital, he assesses the muscle layer, mucosa and perineum before setting the extent of surgery, considering function and recovery together. The final plan is set at an in-person consultation.

Does Dr. Whang Jong-Dae perform revision female genital surgery?

Yes. Dr. Whang Jong-Dae corrects asymmetry, excessive excision, scarring or discomfort remaining after previous surgery. He checks the existing tissue condition first, and if one operation cannot resolve the issue, explains a staged approach in advance.

What materials or techniques does Dr. Whang Jong-Dae use?

Dr. Whang Jong-Dae chooses between laser, vaginal implants and surgical suturing techniques according to each patient's tissue condition. He places importance on thorough antisepsis and reducing pain.

How long should I stay in Korea for surgery with Dr. Whang Jong-Dae?

A stay of eight days and seven nights is recommended for surgery with Dr. Whang Jong-Dae, adjusted according to the extent of surgery and recovery. The exact length is confirmed once the surgical plan is set.

Does Dr. Whang Jong-Dae operate personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Dr. Whang Jong-Dae personally oversees consultation, surgery and aftercare. STMSTYLE has referred patients to him for more than ten years and coordinates initial consultations and scheduling privately from Bangkok. Final decisions are made at an in-person consultation.
