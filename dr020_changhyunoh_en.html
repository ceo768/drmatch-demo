Signature Treatments
Zygoma and Mandible Angle
Precise planning
Reduces cheekbone projection and the mandibular angle to adjust facial width and the profile line. The degree of change the patient wants is heard first, then the osteotomy position and the amount of movement are planned in fine detail.
Three-Part Contouring
Zygoma, mandible angle, chin
All three areas are treated together to adjust the width and length of the whole face. Each area is not viewed separately; the extent of adjustment is set within the overall proportion.
Revision Contouring
Asymmetry and insufficient osteotomy
Corrects left-right asymmetry, or insufficient or excessive osteotomy remaining after previous contouring surgery. The extent of the earlier osteotomy and the state of the bone are checked before the revision plan is made.
The area, extent and number of procedures vary with each individual. The final plan and cost are determined after an in-person consultation. All procedures carry individual variation and possible side effects.
