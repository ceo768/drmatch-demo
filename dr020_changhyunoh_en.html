About the Doctor

Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun is a board-certified plastic surgeon at Banobagi Plastic Surgery in Seoul, known for high-difficulty facial contouring and orthognathic surgery, including revision contouring. A Seoul National University graduate with more than 25 years in practice and over 20,000 cases, he is recognised for precise, result-centred planning that reflects the change each patient wants.

Case Expertise

Zygoma and Mandible Angle Reduction

Reduces cheekbone projection and the mandibular angle to adjust facial width and the profile line. The degree of change the patient wants is heard first, then the osteotomy position and amount of movement are planned in fine detail.

Three-Part Contouring

The zygoma, mandible angle and chin are treated together to adjust the width and length of the whole face, with the extent of adjustment set within the overall proportion rather than area by area.

Orthognathic and Complex Contouring Surgery

Precise, result-centred planning of orthognathic and facial contouring surgery, based on a wide range of cases including orthognathic and contouring work for Let Me In Korea and Thailand.

Revision Contouring

Corrects left-right asymmetry, or insufficient or excessive osteotomy remaining after previous contouring surgery. The extent of the earlier osteotomy and the state of the bone are checked before the revision plan is made.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun perform revision contouring?

Yes. Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun performs revision contouring for issues such as left-right asymmetry or insufficient or excessive osteotomy from previous surgery. He checks the extent of the earlier osteotomy and the state of the bone before planning the revision. The final plan is set at an in-person consultation.

What kind of patients is Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun best suited for?

Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun is best suited to high-difficulty orthognathic and facial contouring patients who want precise planning and a surgeon who listens closely to the change they want. He has more than 25 years in practice and over 20,000 cases.

What materials does Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun use in contouring surgery?

Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun uses titanium pins for bone fixation and silicone where needed. The choice of material depends on each patient's surgical plan.

How long should I stay in Korea for contouring surgery with Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun?

The standard stay for surgery with Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun is eight days and seven nights, adjusted according to the procedure. The exact length is confirmed once the surgical plan is set.

Does Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun operate personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Dr. Oh Chang-Hyun performs the surgery personally. STMSTYLE has worked with him for many years, following Thai patients' consultations and recovery together, and coordinates initial consultations and scheduling from Bangkok. Final decisions are made at an in-person consultation.
