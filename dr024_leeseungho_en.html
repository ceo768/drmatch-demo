About the Doctor

Dr. Lee Seungho is a board-certified otolaryngologist (ENT) and director at BT Plastic Surgery in Seoul, known for cause-based revision rhinoplasty, correction of contracted noses and nasal obstruction, and functional nasal reconstruction. With more than 20 years in practice, he uses 3D CT and nasal endoscopy to identify why a previous surgery failed, then reconstructs both shape and breathing together.

Case Expertise

Cause-based Revision Rhinoplasty

A revision that begins by finding why the previous surgery failed. The material and method used, scarring and adhesion, skin thickness and septal support are checked, and the nasal bone, septum, inferior turbinate and nasal valve are examined by 3D CT and endoscopy before the extent of reconstruction is decided.

Contracted Nose Revision

Corrects a nose that has become lifted or shortened because tissue around an implant has contracted. The implant is removed and adhesion released before the cartilage is repositioned, with missing length and support rebuilt from the tissue required.

Nasal Obstruction Revision and Functional Reconstruction

Corrects obstruction arising after surgery, or existing septal deviation and nasal valve stenosis, reconstructing the airway structure alongside the shape with the aim of restoring comfortable breathing.

Philosophy of Care

His view is that revision rhinoplasty begins not with rebuilding the desired shape but with identifying precisely why the previous surgery failed and what tissue remains. Using 3D CT and nasal endoscopy he examines the nasal bone, septum, deviation, nasal cavity and airway, and treats contracture, adhesion, loss of tip support, implant problems and obstruction as separate issues. After fully explaining what the patient wants and what is actually possible, he decides the material and extent of reconstruction, aiming to restore both a nasal line in harmony with the face and comfortable breathing.

— Lee Seungho

STMSTYLE Assessment

STMSTYLE regards Dr. Lee Seungho as an otolaryngologist who, in revision rhinoplasty, does not simply rebuild the visible line but looks from inside the nose for why the first surgery failed. He distinguishes the causes, including contracture, tip drooping, implant deviation or show-through, a pinched nose and post-surgical obstruction, and varies the tissue and reconstruction method accordingly. STMSTYLE sees him as a particularly compelling option for international patients whose cartilage has run short after several operations, or who need shape and breathing addressed together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Lee Seungho good at revision rhinoplasty?

Yes. Dr. Lee Seungho is known for cause-based revision rhinoplasty. He uses 3D CT and nasal endoscopy to distinguish causes such as contracture, tip drooping, implant problems or obstruction, and applies a different reconstruction for each. The final plan is set at an in-person consultation.

Can Dr. Lee Seungho fix breathing problems after a previous nose job?

Yes. As a board-certified ENT specialist, Dr. Lee Seungho corrects post-surgical obstruction, including septal deviation and nasal valve stenosis, reconstructing the airway structure together with the shape of the nose.

What materials does Dr. Lee Seungho use?

Dr. Lee Seungho chooses between silicone, septal cartilage, ear cartilage, autologous rib cartilage, and autologous dermis and fascia according to damage from previous surgery and the support required. He also offers implant-free rhinoplasty.

How long should I stay in Korea for revision rhinoplasty with Dr. Lee Seungho?

The standard stay for surgery with Dr. Lee Seungho is eight days and seven nights. The clinic's official guidance indicates seven to ten days for suture removal and recovery, adjusted to each patient's condition.

Does Dr. Lee Seungho operate personally, and can I consult through STMSTYLE?

Dr. Lee Seungho performs the surgery personally. STMSTYLE works with BT Plastic Surgery and coordinates initial consultations, scheduling and conditions with the hospital from Bangkok. Final decisions are made at an in-person consultation.
